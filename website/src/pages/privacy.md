# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 30, 2026

**Operator:** EteDrop (“we”, “us”, “our”) — [https://etedrop.com](https://etedrop.com)

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use the EteDrop applications, related services, and our website. By using EteDrop, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Summary

EteDrop is built for **fast file transfer and sharing**. We aim to collect **as little data as possible**. We **do not sell your personal information**. File contents are **not stored long-term on our servers** for normal transfers; signaling and coordination use **temporary server-side state** as described below.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 When you use the EteDrop app

- **Device and session identifiers** — For example, a device identifier and short-lived pairing or session codes so our signaling service can connect sender and receiver (including WebRTC where used).
- **Device name** — If you set or we display a device name for discovery or transfer UI.
- **Technical and connection data** — Such as IP address, network-related signals, timestamps, and diagnostic messages needed to operate signaling, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot reliability.
- **Content you choose to send** — Files, text, or clipboard content you explicitly transfer. This is processed **to complete the transfer** (including any short-lived relay or buffering required by the protocol).

We **do not require you to create an account** to use core transfer features.

### 2.2 Data stored on your device

The app may store **preferences and local data** (for example, settings, remembered devices on local networks) using platform storage such as **SharedPreferences** or equivalent. This stays on your device unless you share it through a transfer.

### 2.3 When you use our website (etedrop.com)

- **Essential cookies / similar technologies** — For basic site operation where needed.
- **Analytics** — We use **Google Analytics** (Google LLC) on the site, and **Baidu Tongji** (Baidu) in some deployments or regions. These services may receive **IP address, device/browser information, and usage events** as described in their respective policies.
- **Server logs** — Standard web logs (e.g., IP, user agent, request path, time).

---

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Provide file transfer, sharing, and related features (including signaling and connectivity).
- Operate, secure, and improve our services; detect and mitigate abuse.
- Understand aggregate usage of our **website** through analytics.

We **do not use your data for third-party advertising** or for building advertising profiles.

---

## 4. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable, we rely on:

- **Performance of a contract / steps at your request** — To deliver transfers you initiate.
- **Legitimate interests** — For example, security, fraud prevention, service improvement, and **website** analytics, balanced against your rights.
- **Consent** — Where required for optional processing (such as certain analytics or notifications, depending on your settings and jurisdiction).

---

## 5. Retention

- **Signaling / session state** — Kept only as long as needed for an active session or short timeouts (for example, inactive device or session expiry), then removed from our servers.
- **File content** — Not kept as permanent storage on our servers for standard end-to-end transfer flows; any temporary buffering is **short-lived**.
- **Server and security logs** — May be kept for a **limited period** (for example, days to weeks) for operations and security, unless a longer period is required by law.
- **Analytics** — Retained according to the analytics provider’s settings and policies.

---

## 6. Sharing and processors

We **do not sell** your personal information.

We share data only with:

- **Infrastructure and service providers** that host or help run our services (servers, networking, email).
- **Analytics providers** as described in Section 2.3 (e.g., Google, Baidu).
- **Authorities** when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

---

## 7. International transfers

If you use EteDrop from outside the country where our servers or vendors operate, your information may be **processed in other countries**. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) or rely on other permitted mechanisms.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including **encryption in transit** where applicable, such as HTTPS and secure real-time channels). **No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.**

## 10. Children’s privacy

EteDrop is **not directed at children under 13** (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 11. Permissions (Android)

Our Android app may request permissions needed for core functionality, such as:

- **Internet**, **network state**, **Wi‑Fi state**, and **Wi‑Fi multicast** — For connectivity and local discovery.
- **Storage** (read/write/manage external storage, as applicable to your Android version) — To access and save files you choose to send or receive.
- **Notifications** — For transfer or background activity notices where supported.
- **Foreground service** — To keep time-sensitive transfer or connectivity tasks reliable when the app is in the background.

We use these permissions **only** to provide the features you use, not for unrelated advertising.
